## T1. Hakata Yatai Night Crawl（博多屋台ナイト）

- **slug**: `hakata-yatai-night`
- **Price**: $95 / person（food & drinks at the first two stalls included）
- **Duration**: 3 hours / 18:30–21:30
- **Meeting point**: Nakasu-Kawabata Station, Exit 1（中洲川端駅1番出口）【要確認】
- **Group**: private, 2–6 guests

**Tagline**: Two yatai, one night, zero guesswork — Fukuoka's open-air food stalls with a local friend.

**What you'll do**
1. Meet Yuhei and walk the Nakasu riverside as the stalls light up.
2. Stall 1: grab two seats at the counter, order Hakata ramen or oden with a cold beer — Yuhei handles the Japanese.
3. Learn the yatai rules locals follow (and the three things not to do).
4. Stall 2: try a skewer or a stall-owner's specialty and chat with the people next to you.
5. Finish with tips for the rest of your Kyushu trip — where to eat, what to skip.

**Included**: local guide · one dish + one drink per person at each of the first two stalls · a short "how to yatai" guide sent by WhatsApp after the tour
**Not included**: extra food and drinks you order · transport to the meeting point
**Good to know**: stalls close in heavy rain or strong wind — we'll move you to an indoor izakaya or refund in full. Vegetarian options are limited; tell us in advance.

**Reviews to quote（Airbnb, Apr 2025, ★5）**

- "It was so much fun. He's friendly and speak English well. His recommendations is all good. You don't have to worry, just enjoy it." — Jean, South Korea
- "Fun experience and a great way to make new friends!" — Danh, Austin TX

## T2. Dazaifu & Yanagawa Day（太宰府・柳川の1日）

- **slug**: `dazaifu-yanagawa`
- **Price**: $180 / person
- **Duration**: 8 hours / 9:00–17:00
- **Meeting point**: Nishitetsu Fukuoka (Tenjin) Station ticket gate（西鉄福岡（天神）駅 改札）【要確認】
- **Group**: private, 2–6 guests

**Tagline**: Shrines in the morning, a canal boat in the afternoon — the classic Fukuoka day trip, without the tour bus.

**What you'll do**
1. Ride the Nishitetsu line to Dazaifu with Yuhei.
2. Walk the approach to Dazaifu Tenmangu, Japan's shrine of learning, and try a warm umegae-mochi rice cake.
3. Continue by train to Yanagawa, the "water town" of Kyushu.
4. Glide through the old castle moats on a traditional punt boat.
5. Lunch on Yanagawa's famous steamed eel rice (unagi no seiro-mushi), then ride back to Tenjin.

**Included**: local guide for the whole day · help buying the Dazaifu–Yanagawa sightseeing ticket
**Not included**: the Nishitetsu Dazaifu–Yanagawa sightseeing ticket (rail + canal boat, about ¥3,620 / adult in 2026) · lunch
**Good to know**: wear comfortable shoes; the canal boat runs about an hour and is uncovered in summer.

運営メモ
- 「太宰府・柳川観光きっぷ」（2026年6月時点 デジタル3,620円）に電車と川下りが含まれる。お客様が購入（[CROSSROAD FUKUOKA](https://www.crossroadfukuoka.jp/feature/dazaifu_yanagawa)）
- 博多港のクルーズ客向けにはS1で8時間版を使う

## T3. Nagasaki Heritage Day（長崎の世界遺産）

- **slug**: `nagasaki-heritage`
- **Price**: $220 / person
- **Duration**: 9 hours / 8:30–17:30 from Hakata（or 7 hours from Nagasaki Station）
- **Meeting point**: JR Hakata Station, Central Gate（博多駅 中央改札）【要確認】
- **Group**: private, 2–6 guests

**Tagline**: 400 years of Japan's window to the world in one day — churches, merchants' houses and the city that rebuilt itself.

**What you'll do**
1. Take the Kamome line from Hakata to Nagasaki (about 1 h 20 min with one easy transfer).
2. Visit Oura Church, part of a UNESCO World Heritage site, and Glover Garden above the harbor.
3. Lunch on Nagasaki champon in Chinatown.
4. Walk Dejima, the island where Japan traded with Europe for two centuries.
5. Pause at the Peace Park and Atomic Bomb Museum before heading back.

**Included**: local guide for the whole day · train timing and seat planning
**Not included**: train fare · admission fees · lunch
**Good to know**: the Atomic Bomb Museum can be emotional; we can shorten or skip it.

運営メモ
- 博多〜長崎はリレーかもめ＋西九州新幹線で最速1時間20分（[日本旅行](https://www.nta.co.jp/media/tripa/articles/6BpUG)）
- 大浦天主堂 8:00〜18:00（受付17:30まで）。入場料は公式で確認【要確認】
- 長崎港のクルーズ客向けにはS1（長崎版）として現地発7時間で使う

## T4. Yufuin & Beppu Onsen Day（由布院・別府の温泉の1日）

- **slug**: `yufuin-beppu-onsen`
- **Price**: $240 / person
- **Duration**: 11 hours / 8:00–19:00 from Hakata
- **Meeting point**: JR Hakata Station, Central Gate【要確認】
- **Group**: private, 2–4 guests（train seats）

**Tagline**: Japan's hot-spring capital in a day — a forest train, a lakeside village and the steaming "hells" of Beppu.

**What you'll do**
1. Ride the Yufuin no Mori, a wood-paneled sightseeing train (about 2 hours).
2. Stroll Yufuin's lanes to Lake Kinrin, where mist rises off the water on cool mornings.
3. Lunch and an optional private onsen bath in Yufuin.
4. Bus to Beppu and walk the Jigoku, boiling ponds in red, blue and white.
5. Return to Hakata on the Sonic express.

**Included**: local guide for the whole day · train and bus planning
**Not included**: train and bus fares · admission and bath fees · lunch
**Good to know**: Yufuin no Mori seats open one month ahead and sell out in autumn and winter — book as early as you can.
**Want Aso too?** Aso Caldera is a separate custom day by car; ask us.

運営メモ
- ゆふいんの森は約2時間、指定席は1ヶ月前に発売で埋まりやすい（[trvlogue](https://www.trvlogue.com/ja/yufuin-no-mori-train-guide)）。予約の確定後すぐに座席を押さえる
- 阿蘇は公共交通では1日で回りにくい。車が必要なため、阿蘇を含む日はカスタム（運転手付きの車は登録済みの業者に依頼）

**Tagline**: Your port day, your pace — a private local guide waiting at the terminal, back on board in plenty of time.

**What you'll do**
1. Meet Yuhei at the cruise terminal exit with a JourneyMate sign.
2. Choose your route: Dazaifu & Yanagawa or Fukuoka city from Hakata; churches, Glover Garden and Chinatown in Nagasaki.
3. Skip the crowds of the ship's bus tours and eat where locals eat.
4. Be back at the port at least two hours before all-aboard.

**Included**: local guide · terminal meet-and-greet · route planned around your ship's schedule
**Not included**: taxi or train fares · admission fees · lunch
**Good to know**: send us your ship name and date when you book; we check the port schedule for you.
