**説明文（What you'll do）**

```
Fukuoka's yatai are tiny open-air food stalls with about ten seats each — and the best way to spend your first night in the city.

I'm Yuhei, a local guide based in Fukuoka. I'll take you to two stalls along the Nakasu river, get us seats at the counter, and order for you: Hakata ramen, oden, skewers, the owner's specialty. Your first dish and drink at each stall are included.

Along the way you'll learn the yatai rules locals follow, practice a few easy Japanese phrases, and get my tips for the rest of your Kyushu trip.

Past guests: "You don't have to worry, just enjoy it."
```

**含まれるもの**：屋台2軒での1品＋1ドリンク（1人あたり）／ツアー後に「屋台の楽しみ方」ガイドをWhatsAppで送付
**ゲストの持ち物**：現金（追加注文用、屋台は現金のみの店も多い）

## 2. Viator（Tripadvisor Experiences）

サプライヤー登録 → 商品ごとに以下を入力。手数料は20〜35%（[Magpie](https://about.magpie.travel/how-to-work-with/tripadvisor)）なので、Viator価格は直販価格＋25%前後に設定し、直販が最安になるようにする。

| 商品 | Viator掲載価格（目安） | カテゴリ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hakata Yatai Night Crawl | $119 / person | Food & Drink Tours |
| Dazaifu & Yanagawa Private Day Tour | $225 / person | Private Day Trips |
| Kyushu Shore Excursion from Hakata or Nagasaki Port | $275 / person | Shore Excursions |

**Shore Excursionの概要文（Overview, 〜500字）**

```
Skip the crowded ship tours and spend your port day in Fukuoka or Nagasaki with a private local guide.

Yuhei meets you at the cruise terminal and plans the day around your ship's schedule: Dazaifu Tenmangu shrine and a canal boat in Yanagawa from Hakata Port, or Oura Church, Glover Garden and Chinatown from Nagasaki Port. You eat where locals eat and move at your own pace.

You are back at the port at least two hours before all-aboard. Private for your group of 2–6.
```

**必須項目メモ**：キャンセル規定（Viatorの標準「24時間前まで無料」に合わせるか要判断。直販は72時間）、乗船時刻の入力欄を「予約時の質問」に追加、ピックアップ地点は「博多港クルーズセンター」「長崎港 松が枝ターミナル」【名称を要確認】。
