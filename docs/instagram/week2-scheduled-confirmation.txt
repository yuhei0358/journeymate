Meet oden: a warm yatai favorite 🏮
Ingredients simmer in dashi broth. Egg and daikon are common choices, but each stall has its own selection. The yellow paste on the side may be Japanese mustard—start with a little.

One ramen word to remember: kaedama 🍜
At Hakata ramen shops that offer it, kaedama means an extra serving of noodles for your remaining broth. Keep some soup and check the menu for availability and price.
